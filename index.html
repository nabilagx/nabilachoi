PANDA — Patient Ambient Notification & Danger Analysis

An end-to-end healthcare IoT system engineered for bedridden stroke patients—combining multi-sensor ambient monitoring, telemetry streaming over HiveMQ MQTT, and a Random Forest classifier paired with automated AC climate intervention.

The Problem

Paralyzed stroke patients cannot verbalize thermal stress or agitation, triggering acute cardiovascular distress spikes.

The Solution

ESP32 sensor fusion feeding a Random Forest model that activates an IR blaster to modulate AC target state automatically.
